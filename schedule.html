8–10 hour sleep window

Sleep is a performance priority for a growing athlete. Friday practice ends late, so Saturday morning stays flexible for recovery.

Fuel, don't fast

Eat before and after lifting or practice. A 16-year-old athlete should not use fasting or an aggressive calorie deficit to chase a lower body-fat percentage.

10-min mobility reset

Ankles, calves, hips, and upper back can be moved daily for comfort and posture; mobility does not lengthen bones or guarantee extra height.

Respect the weekly load

Two strength days, one focused jump day, two volleyball practices, and recovery days are enough. Reduce extra work during tournaments or soreness.
