Training Plan

Growth-plate-safe split · vertical jump · lipid-clearing cardio

Weekly program — day-by-day workouts

At-home mode — yoga mat + 2×20 lb dumbbells only

MONDay 1 · Upper — V-Taper
  • 4×8–10Wide-Grip Pull-ups / Lat Pulldowns
  • 3×8–12Incline DB Bench Press (30°)
  • 3×10–12Seated Cable Rows (close grip)
  • 4×12–15DB Lateral Raises
  • 3×10–12DB Hammer Curls
  • 3×15Face Pulls

Width day — lats, side delts, rear delts. Shoulders down on pulls, squeeze the lats at the bottom.

TUEDay 2 · Lower — Quad & Core
  • 4×10–12/legBulgarian Split Squats (DB)
  • 3×10–12DB Romanian Deadlifts
  • 3×12–15Leg Extensions
  • 4×15Standing Calf Raises
  • 3×12–15Hanging Knee Raises

Torso upright on split squats to bias quads; 1-s squeeze at the top of extensions; no swinging on hangs.

WEDDay 3 · Active Recovery
  • 10,000Steps (minimum)
  • 20–30 minLISS — incline walk 3.0 mph @ 8% or bike, HR 120–140
  • 15 minPosture & Decompression Routine

Recovery day, not a training day — keep it easy, keep moving, keep the heart rate low.

THUDay 4 · Upper — Thickness & Arms
  • 4×8–10Flat DB Bench Press
  • 3×10–12Chest-Supported DB Rows
  • 3×10–12Seated DB Shoulder Press
  • 3×8–12Bodyweight Dips (assisted if needed)
  • 3×10–12Incline DB Curls
  • 3×12–15Cable Lateral Raises

Tuck elbows 45° on press; chest-supported rows keep the spine neutral — growth-plate safe.

FRIDay 5 · Lower — Posterior Chain
  • 4×10–12Leg Press
  • 3×12–15Lying Leg Curls
  • 3×12/legWalking DB Lunges
  • 3×12–15Seated Calf Raises
  • 3×8–10Ab Wheel Rollouts (or plank shoulder taps)

Slow eccentric on curls; keep the lower back on the pad on leg press; rollouts in posterior pelvic tilt.

SATDay 6 · Active Recovery
  • 10,000Steps (minimum)
  • 15 minPosture & Decompression Routine
  • optional20–30 min LISS if steps fall short

The week's work is done — recovery drives the gains. Keep HR low.

SUNDay 7 · Full Rest
  • restNo training — full physical rest
  • 15 minPosture & Decompression Routine (before bed)
  • 8.5–9.5 hSleep — GH pulses do the work

Shutdown by 10 PM. No screens, cool room — protect the growth-hormone window.

MONDay 1 · Upper — V-Taper
  • 4×8–10DB Pullovers (yoga mat)
  • 3×8–12DB Floor Press (yoga mat)
  • 3×10–12Bent-Over DB Rows (torso braced on bed)
  • 4×12–15DB Lateral Raises
  • 3×10–12DB Hammer Curls
  • 3×15Reverse DB Flys (bent over)

Width day — lats, side delts, rear delts. Pullovers open the lats; keep shoulders down on rows; slow 2-s lowering on flys.

TUEDay 2 · Lower — Quad & Core
  • 4×10–12/legBulgarian Split Squats (DBs, rear foot on chair)
  • 3×10–12DB Romanian Deadlifts
  • 3×12–15Goblet Squats (DB racked at chest)
  • 4×15/legSingle-Leg Calf Raises (on stair)
  • 3×12–15Dead Bugs (yoga mat)

Torso upright on split squats to bias quads; hold the DB to your chest on goblets; lower back glued to the mat on dead bugs.

WEDDay 3 · Active Recovery
  • 10,000Steps (minimum)
  • 20–30 minLISS — brisk walk or bike, HR 120–140
  • 15 minPosture & Decompression Routine

Recovery day, not a training day — keep it easy, keep moving, keep the heart rate low.

THUDay 4 · Upper — Thickness & Arms
  • 4×8–10Wide Push-Ups (feet elevated)
  • 3×10–12One-Arm DB Rows (opposite hand on mat)
  • 3×10–12Seated DB Shoulder Press
  • 3×8–12Diamond Push-Ups (yoga mat)
  • 3×10–12Alternating DB Curls
  • 3×12–15DB Lateral Raises (slow tempo)

Tuck elbows 45° on push-ups; press-ups replace dips — slow eccentric, full lockout; squeeze at the top of curls.

FRIDay 5 · Lower — Posterior Chain
  • 4×10–12DB Sumo Squats (goblet hold)
  • 3×12–15/legSingle-Leg DB Romanian Deadlifts
  • 3×12/legDB Reverse Lunges (yoga mat)
  • 3×12–15/legSingle-Leg Calf Raises (DB in hand)
  • 3×8–10Plank Shoulder Taps (yoga mat)

Slow eccentric on RDLs; keep chest proud and spine neutral; core braced on plank taps — no hip sway.

Resistance — growth-plate safe

  • Removed: heavy barbell back squats & deadlifts (epiphyseal plate protection)
  • Replaced with: goblet squats, split squats, leg presses, chest-supported rows
  • Double-progression model, 1–2 RIR, on a 4-day upper/lower split
  • Hypertrophy focus — muscle retention while cutting
  • 30–40 g protein + 50–80 g carbs within 90 min post-lift

Plyometrics & conditioning

  • 12-week vertical jump program — volume escalates 6 → 7 days/week
  • Broad jumps · lateral bounds · sprint intervals · jump rope
  • 50–55 min brisk morning walks, 3–4×/week (BCAA intra-cardio)
  • Cardio skipped on heavy lift days
  • Volleyball conditioning — lateral speed + explosive reach for matches

Daily 15-min Height Unlock sequence

Passive Dead Hangs Thoracic Extensions (foam roller) Cobra Stretches Wall Slides Hip Flexor Stretches RKC Planks

Corrects anterior pelvic tilt, thoracic kyphosis, and rounded shoulders — recovering 0.5–1.5" of hidden height. Baseline: 5'8" · 152 lbs · BMI 23.1.
