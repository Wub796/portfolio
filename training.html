Strength rules for a growing athlete

  • Supervision first: learn each lift from a qualified coach or adult; no 1-rep max testing
  • Quality loading: use 2-3 reps in reserve, full control, and pain-free range of motion
  • Progress slowly: add one rep or the smallest load only after every rep is crisp
  • Schedule: 2 full-body lifts plus 1 upper/jump session; reduce volume during tournaments
  • Stop and report: sharp pain, swelling, limping, or persistent joint pain needs an adult and clinician

Jump plan + volleyball load

  • Two focused jump drills: Tuesday only, about 30-35 total contacts including the warm-up hops
  • Practices count: Friday and Sunday already provide high-intensity jumping and conditioning
  • Landing: quiet feet, knees track over toes, hips back, and reset between attempts
  • No junk volume: skip extra jump rope, sprints, or max-jump challenges after practice
  • Track weekly: record the best of three jumps when fresh, not every day
